# Linear Equation in One Variable — Narration Script & Storyboard Timing

- **Topic:** Solving Linear Equations in One Variable ($2x + 3 = 11$)
- **Target Audience:** School students, beginners, YouTube Shorts & Instagram Reels
- **Format:** 9:16 Vertical (1080 × 1920), 30 FPS
- **Total Duration:** Exactly 60.0 Seconds

---

## Storyboard & Narration Breakdown

### Scene 1: The Hook
- **Timestamp:** 00:00 – 00:05 (5.0s)
- **Visual Content:**
  - Header badge: `"LINEAR EQUATION"`
  - Prominent equation: $2x + 3 = 11$
  - Variable $x$ highlighted in vibrant Cyan ($2$, $3$, $11$ in Yellow, $+$ in Orange)
  - Visual focus circumscribing the equation
- **On-Screen Subtitle:** `"Can you find the value of x?"`
- **Narration:**
  > *"Can you find the value of x?"*

---

### Scene 2: Understanding Linear Equations
- **Timestamp:** 00:05 – 00:12 (7.0s)
- **Visual Content:**
  - $2x + 3 = 11$ remains centered
  - A Cyan bracket / brace targets $x$ with the label `"Highest Power = 1"`
  - Callout badge: `"Degree 1 → Linear"`
- **On-Screen Subtitle:** `"This is a linear equation because the variable's highest power is one."`
- **Narration:**
  > *"This is a linear equation because the variable's highest power is one."*

---

### Scene 3: Subtract 3 from Both Sides
- **Timestamp:** 00:12 – 00:24 (12.0s)
- **Visual Content:**
  - Step badge: `"STEP 1: ISOLATE VARIABLE"`
  - Symmetrical balance scale appears below with left and right pans in equilibrium
  - Equation expands to: $2x + 3 - 3 = 11 - 3$
  - Red cancellation slashes over $+3$ and $-3$ on the left side
  - Equation simplifies to: $2x = 8$
- **On-Screen Subtitle:** `"To isolate x, subtract 3 from both sides. The plus 3 cancels, leaving 2x = 8."`
- **Narration:**
  > *"To isolate x, subtract three from both sides. The plus three cancels, leaving two x equals eight."*

---

### Scene 4: Divide Both Sides by 2
- **Timestamp:** 00:24 – 00:34 (10.0s)
- **Visual Content:**
  - Step badge: `"STEP 2: SOLVE FOR X"`
  - Fraction division appears: $\frac{2x}{2} = \frac{8}{2}$
  - Coefficients divide out, transforming into the final answer: $x = 4$
  - Bright neon green surrounding box illuminates $x = 4$
- **On-Screen Subtitle:** `"Now divide both sides by 2. So x equals 4!"`
- **Narration:**
  > *"Now divide both sides by two. So x equals four."*

---

### Scene 5: Verify the Answer
- **Timestamp:** 00:34 – 00:44 (10.0s)
- **Visual Content:**
  - Step badge: `"CHECK & VERIFY"`
  - Original equation recalled: $2x + 3 = 11$
  - Substitution of $x = 4$: $2(4) + 3 = 11$
  - Arithmetic simplification: $8 + 3 = 11$
  - Final verification: $11 = 11$ highlighted in bright green with a checkmark badge
- **On-Screen Subtitle:** `"Let's check: 2(4) + 3 = 11 → 11 = 11. Both sides match!"`
- **Narration:**
  > *"Let's check. Two times four plus three equals eleven. Both sides match!"*

---

### Scene 6: Key Takeaway
- **Timestamp:** 00:44 – 00:51 (7.0s)
- **Visual Content:**
  - Golden Rule banner: `"THE GOLDEN RULE OF ALGEBRA"`
  - Core principle: `"Whatever you do to one side, do to the other!"`
  - Balance scale visual reinforces keeping equations in equilibrium
- **On-Screen Subtitle:** `"Remember: Whatever you do to one side, do to the other."`
- **Narration:**
  > *"Remember, whatever you do to one side, do to the other."*

---

### Scene 7: Audience Challenge
- **Timestamp:** 00:51 – 01:00 (9.0s)
- **Visual Content:**
  - Challenge banner: `"YOUR TURN!"`
  - New equation revealed: $3x - 2 = 10$
  - Call-to-action cards: `"Can YOU solve it?"` and `"Comment your answer below! 👇"`
  - Answer is kept secret to drive viewer comments and engagement
- **On-Screen Subtitle:** `"Your turn! Solve 3x - 2 = 10. Comment your answer below!"`
- **Narration:**
  > *"Your turn! Solve three x minus two equals ten. Comment your answer!"*
